Frequently Asked

Got Questions?
We've Got Answers.

Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us directly →

What services does CodeCraft Ghana offer? ▾
We build websites and online stores, custom software and business systems, and ProDesk24 setups. We also run digital marketing, branding and design, IT consulting and training, and hosting and maintenance. Starting prices are published in Ghana cedis on the Services page.
Do you work with businesses outside Ghana? ▾
Absolutely. We work with clients across West Africa and internationally — including Ghanaian diaspora businesses in the UK, USA, Canada, and Europe, as well as companies looking to enter the Ghanaian and African market.
What types of websites do you develop? ▾
Starter websites and stores, landing pages, business and corporate sites, booking sites, membership or directory sites, custom portals, and larger e-commerce builds. Standard packages include a mobile-responsive build, basic SEO, and domain, SSL, and hosting for the first year.
How much does a website cost? ▾
A Starter Website or Starter Online Store is GH₵2,000. A Business Website is GH₵4,500. Premium E-Commerce is GH₵8,500. Larger portals and custom systems are quoted after discovery, from GH₵10,000 upward. All list prices are in Ghana cedis and exclude taxes. Use the calculator for a starting figure, then we confirm it in a written quotation.
Can I pay in Ghanaian Cedis (GHS)? ▾
Yes. All quotes and payments are in Ghanaian Cedis (GHS). We support Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo), debit/credit card, and bank transfer on our Payment page via Paystack.
Do you offer payment plans? ▾
Yes. Up to GH₵5,000: 70% deposit and 30% before handover. GH₵5,001–15,000: 60% deposit, 30% at a milestone, and 10% before handover. GH₵15,001–50,000: 50%, 30%, and 20%. Above GH₵50,000, the milestones are written into the contract. Work starts after the deposit clears, and final files are released after the agreed balance is paid. Marketing and maintenance plans are billed monthly in advance.
What is your process from start to finish? ▾
Our process has four stages: (1) Discovery — we learn your goals and requirements; (2) Strategy — we propose a clear plan with timelines and deliverables; (3) Execution — we build, test, and refine with your feedback; (4) Launch & Grow — we deploy and stay on to support and optimise.
How long does a typical project take? ▾
A Starter Website is usually 7–10 days, a Starter Online Store 10–14 days, a Business Website 2–3 weeks, and Premium E-Commerce 4–6 weeks. Those clocks start once the deposit and your content are both in. Custom systems are quoted with their own timeline. Please send content within 7 days of the deposit and feedback within 5 business days of each review.
How involved will I need to be during the project? ▾
We make it as painless as possible. You'll typically need to provide content (text, images, branding), review designs at 2–3 checkpoints, and give final approval. Most clients spend 2–4 hours total on their end for a standard website.
What is ProDesk24? ▾
ProDesk24 is our all-in-one business management platform built for African SMEs. It includes CRM, invoicing, project management, and a customer support helpdesk — all in one affordable, easy-to-use tool. Try it free at prodesk24.com.
What is Zone? ▾
Zone is our curated online marketplace where Ghanaian businesses can list and sell their products. It's designed to help local businesses reach more customers online without needing to build their own store from scratch.
Do you offer support after a project is delivered? ▾
Yes. Every project includes 30 days of defect fixes for the agreed scope. A Business Website includes 3 months of support, Premium E-Commerce includes 6 months, and a custom system includes 3 months. After that, website maintenance starts from GH₵250 a month. New features are quoted separately.
How do I reach you for urgent support? ▾
Email us at hello@codecraftgh.com or WhatsApp us on +233 549 739 010. For clients on a retainer, you'll have access to a dedicated support channel with guaranteed response times.

2. Services

CodeCraft Ghana provides websites and e-commerce, custom software and systems, ProDesk24 and business automation, digital marketing, branding and design, IT consulting, training, office IT, and hosting, maintenance, and support. A written quotation defines the scope, price, and timeline for each engagement.

  • Any changes to the agreed scope must be requested in writing and may result in additional charges and timeline adjustments.
  • We reserve the right to decline any project request at our discretion.
  • Timelines are estimates and may be affected by client responsiveness, third-party dependencies, or unforeseen technical challenges.

3. Payment Terms

Payment terms are outlined in each project proposal. General terms are as follows:

  • Prices are in Ghana cedis and exclude taxes unless the quotation says otherwise.
  • Up to GH₵5,000: 70% deposit and 30% before handover. GH₵5,001 to GH₵15,000: 60% deposit, 30% at a milestone, and 10% before handover. GH₵15,001 to GH₵50,000: 50%, 30%, and 20%. Above GH₵50,000, milestones are set in the contract.
  • No work starts until the deposit has cleared into an official CodeCraft payment channel.
  • Marketing, maintenance, and support plans are billed monthly in advance and are due by the 5th of the month. Marketing retainers have a minimum term of three months.
  • Domain and hosting renewals are billed at least 30 days before expiry. After the included first year, website renewals are GH₵400–800.
  • After 14 days overdue, work pauses and a late-payment charge of 2% per month may apply to the overdue balance. Hosting may be suspended after 14 days’ written notice.
  • We accept Mobile Money, debit or credit card, and bank transfer. Payments are in Ghana cedis.
  • A deposit is not refundable once work has started, because it covers reserved time and work already done. If you cancel, work completed and committed third-party costs remain payable. A refund is considered only if CodeCraft cancels or does not deliver the agreed scope.
  • Standard package quotations are valid for 30 days. Custom work and retainers are valid for 14 days unless the quotation says otherwise.

4. Intellectual Property

On full payment, you own the final content, branding, and data created for the project, and you receive a perpetual licence to use the delivered website or system.

  • Until the agreed payment is received, CodeCraft holds administrative credentials, source files, and domain control.
  • CodeCraft keeps ownership of its pre-existing tools, frameworks, libraries, and reusable components, and licenses them to you as part of the deliverable.
  • Transfer of full source-code ownership is available on request and is quoted per project.
  • Third-party themes, plugins, fonts, and stock assets stay with their vendors and follow their licences.
  • A “Developed by CodeCraft Technologies” footer credit is standard. Removal is by written agreement.
  • We may show completed work in our portfolio unless confidentiality is agreed in writing.

  • We make no warranty that our services will be uninterrupted or error-free, though we strive for the highest quality in all our work.

7. Termination

Either party may terminate a project engagement with 14 days' written notice. In the event of termination:

  • The client pays for work completed to the termination date and for third-party costs already committed.
  • A deposit is not refundable once work has started. It is refundable only if CodeCraft cancels or does not deliver the agreed scope.
  • Work produced to date is handed over once the outstanding agreed payments are received.
  • A project with no client response for 30 days is paused. After 60 days it may be closed, and restarting is quoted again.
  • CodeCraft Ghana may terminate immediately if the client is in material breach of these terms, including non-payment.

In the event of a dispute, both parties agree to first attempt resolution through good-faith negotiation within 30 days of notice.

If negotiation fails, disputes shall be resolved through mediation in Accra, Ghana, under the rules of the Ghana Mediation Centre, before any legal action is commenced.
