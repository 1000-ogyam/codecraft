What we build

The pieces a larger organisation actually needs

A starter website stays on the Services page. This is the work that needs accounts, roles, and a record more than one person uses.

Member and institutional sites

Directories, accounts, search, and a public site the organisation can update. A membership or directory site starts at GH₵8,000. An institutional site with its own theme starts at GH₵15,000.

The system the team uses

Inventory, production, CRM, HR, bookings, or a management suite. ProDesk24 when that product fits. A custom build when it does not. Advanced enterprise software starts at GH₵25,000. A management suite starts at GH₵40,000.

Payments in the right record

Paystack, mobile money, and other gateways, from GH₵2,000 when the integration is not already in the package. The provider’s own fees stay with the organisation.

AI on your own material

An assistant or a draft step that reads your pages, products, or member records. A person still confirms anything that has to be right. Priced from the brief.

Care after launch

Website care from GH₵250 a month. Yearly support for a custom system is about 15% of the build price, for defect fixes, security updates, and small improvements. New features are quoted on their own.

Discovery before a large build

Systems from GH₵15,000 start with technical discovery from GH₵1,000. That fee is credited in full if you proceed within 30 days. Smaller systems include a free discovery session of up to two hours.

How an engagement runs

A written scope before anyone starts building.

The first call is free, up to 30 minutes, and remote wherever possible. On-site visits are from GH₵400 plus transport, and only after that visit is confirmed. Custom quotations are valid for 14 days. Nothing starts until the deposit has cleared, and handover waits until the agreed balance is paid.

From GH₵15,001 to GH₵50,000 the split is 50%, then 30% at a milestone, then 20% before handover. Above GH₵50,000 the milestones are written into the contract. A manufacturing system is typically 8–16 weeks. A management suite is typically 3–6 months. The quotation states the timeline for your scope.

On full payment you own the final content, branding, and data, and you receive a licence to use the system. CodeCraft keeps its existing tools and reusable components. Systems that process personal data are scoped with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) in mind.
