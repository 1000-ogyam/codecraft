Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

How quickly can you start on a project?

Typically within 1–2 weeks of signing. We'll do a full discovery session first to make sure we understand your needs before diving into execution.

Do you work with clients outside Ghana?

Absolutely. We work with clients across Africa and internationally — diaspora businesses, pan-African brands, and global companies entering the Ghanaian market.

What if I don't know exactly what I need?

That's completely fine — and it's more common than you'd think. Book a free consultation and we'll help you figure out the right path forward.

Do you offer ongoing support after a project launches?

Yes. Every project includes an initial support period, and we offer monthly maintenance and growth retainers for clients who want a long-term partner.

Can I pay in Ghanaian Cedis?

Yes — we quote and accept payment in Ghanaian Cedis (GHS) via Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash), bank transfer, and card through Paystack.

How long does a typical website take?

A standard business website takes 2–4 weeks from kick-off to launch. More complex platforms and e-commerce builds typically take 6–10 weeks, depending on scope.
