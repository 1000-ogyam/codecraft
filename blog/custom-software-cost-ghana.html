Custom software prices in Ghana

Custom software is priced from the job, not from a single package. Published floors in cedis, excluding tax: POS setup on an existing platform from GH₵5,200. A custom POS and inventory build is GH₵7,000 to GH₵25,000. A CRM is GH₵8,000 to GH₵30,000. A custom business web application starts at GH₵15,000. Advanced enterprise software starts at GH₵25,000. A management suite starts at GH₵40,000.

What every custom system includes

Discovery, a working demo before go-live, team training, and a user guide, plus three months of post-launch support. Hosting for a custom system is billed separately, at cost. Optional yearly support is about 15% of the build price and covers defect fixes, security updates, and small improvements.

Discovery

Systems under GH₵15,000 include a free discovery session of up to two hours. Systems of GH₵15,000 and above start with paid technical discovery from GH₵1,000. That fee is credited in full if you proceed within 30 days.
