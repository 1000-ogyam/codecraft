When a Ghanaian business needs an ERP suite

An ERP, in plain language, is one system for the records several departments already keep apart: stock, sales, finance, and the people who approve them. A business management suite starts at GH₵40,000. The published timeline is 3 to 6 months. Prices exclude tax.

Do the smaller system first when

Only one of those records hurts. Inventory from GH₵10,000, a CRM from GH₵8,000, or ProDesk24 onboarding from GH₵1,000 may be the whole job. A suite is justified when the same item must be visible to production, the store, and the invoice without a weekly export.

How a project of this size is paid

From GH₵15,001 to GH₵50,000 the split is 50 percent, then 30 percent, then 20 percent before handover. Above GH₵50,000 the milestones are in the contract. Discovery from GH₵1,000 is credited if you proceed within 30 days.
