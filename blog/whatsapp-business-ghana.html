WhatsApp follow-up for customer enquiries

Most Ghanaian enquiries already start on WhatsApp. The website’s job is often to catch the people who will not chat first, and to give the chat a page to point at. An assistant on WhatsApp that answers from your content is scoped as AI work, not as a fixed package.

What the brief must include

The questions you are willing to answer automatically, the questions that must reach a person, and the documents the assistant may use. Prices, delivery areas, and refunds should be copied from a source you control. The assistant should not invent them.

The page still needs to exist

A starter website is GH₵2,000 if there is nowhere for the chat to send a serious buyer. Tracking so you can see which page led to the chat is GH₵750 when that setup is separate.
