What a premium online store includes

GH₵8,500 is the published price of the premium store. It is the package for a business that will live in the shop, not a one-page catalogue. Prices exclude tax.

Read the package against your shop

Use the Services page for the line items in that package, then tell us where your shop is different. Common differences are a payment method, a stock file that must be imported, and more products than the package includes. Extra products are GH₵5 each. A gateway that is not already included starts at GH₵2,000.

After it opens

Included support on a premium store is 6 months for defects in the agreed scope, security updates, how-to guidance, and minor content edits of up to 2 hours a month. New features are quoted on their own. Ongoing care plans start at GH₵250 a month.
