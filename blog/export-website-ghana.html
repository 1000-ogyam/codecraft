Websites for Ghanaian exporters

A buyer in another country will not call a Ghana number as the first step. The site has to show the product and offer a form or an email that reaches a person who can answer. A starter site is GH₵2,000. A business website is GH₵4,500. Prices exclude tax. Year-one domain, SSL, and hosting are included on those packages.

What to put on the page

  • The product, the pack, and what you do not ship.
  • A way to ask for a specification or a sample.
  • Proof you are allowed to show: photos, certificates you hold, and named clients only with permission.
