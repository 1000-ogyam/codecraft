The Data Protection Act and a business website

If the site or system stores names, phone numbers, payment references, pupil records, or staff files, the design has to say who can see them. Systems that process personal data are scoped with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) in mind.

What belongs in the scope

  • What is collected, and why that field is needed.
  • Who on your team can open a record.
  • How long you intend to keep it, in plain language you can stand behind.
  • How a person asks for a correction.

A privacy policy page is not a substitute for those decisions. Our own privacy policy describes this website and ProDesk24. Your customers need a policy that matches your business. Copywriting for a page starts at GH₵300 if you want that page written.
