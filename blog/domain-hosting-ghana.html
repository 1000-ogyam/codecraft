Domain, SSL, and hosting for a new website

On a starter website, a starter store, and a business website, the first year of the domain, SSL, and hosting is included in the published price. You should not see a second invoice for those three items in year one unless the quotation says the name or the plan is outside that inclusion.

Year two

Domain and hosting renewals are billed at least 30 days before expiry. Website renewals in the published range are GH₵400 to GH₵800 a year, depending on the plan. Custom-system hosting is billed at cost, separately from the build.

Email

Business email setup on its own is GH₵300. It can be added to a website. The mailbox host’s own fee, if there is one, is stated on the quote.
