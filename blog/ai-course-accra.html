AI courses at the Accra studio

The courses are short and practical. Students, and youth or people already in IT, train for up to six weeks. In person is GH₵500 a week, with four seats. Online is GH₵1,000 once, and online seats are not capped. Prices exclude tax.

Business owners

Owners spend one weekend on using AI and a system to follow work from production or inventory to the sale. ProDesk24 is the system in the room. In person is GH₵1,500, paid at the front desk, with four seats. Online is GH₵1,000, paid with Paystack.

After school

Students who finish can intern with CodeCraft, and CodeCraft accepts National Service personnel after school. That is company acceptance. It is not a government posting. A free introduction is separate from the paid seat. If the four in-person seats for the week or weekend are full, the next period is the option. A waitlist does not hold a seat.
