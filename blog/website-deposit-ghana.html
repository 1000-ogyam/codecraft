How website deposits work

Up to GH₵5,000 the split is 70 percent deposit and 30 percent before handover. From GH₵5,001 to GH₵15,000 it is 60 percent, then 30 percent at a milestone, then 10 percent before handover. From GH₵15,001 to GH₵50,000 it is 50 percent, 30 percent, and 20 percent. Above GH₵50,000 the milestones are written into the contract.

What the deposit means

Nothing starts until it has cleared. Handover waits until the agreed balance is paid. If you cancel after work has started, the deposit covers reserved time and work already done, and committed third-party costs are still payable.

How long the quote lasts

Standard package quotations are valid for 30 days. Custom work and retainers are valid for 14 days. Prices exclude tax unless the quotation says otherwise.
