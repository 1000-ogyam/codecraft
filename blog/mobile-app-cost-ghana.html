What a mobile app costs in Ghana

A mobile application starts at GH₵25,000, excluding tax. The quote follows the stores you must ship to, the accounts, and whether the app is the product or a window onto a system you already have.

Build the site first when

Customers need to read, buy, or book, and a phone browser is enough. A starter website or store is GH₵2,000. A booking website is GH₵6,000. A portal with logins starts at GH₵10,000. An app is the right spend when the phone itself must do something a browser will not: a camera workflow, offline capture, or a store listing you have decided is worth the upkeep.

After launch

Store accounts, review delays, and updates when a phone vendor changes the rules are part of the real cost. Yearly support on a custom build is about 15% of the build price if you want that cover. It is optional and quoted.
