POS systems for shops in Ghana

A point of sale is the screen where a sale is recorded and the stock number moves. If a known platform already does that, implementation starts at GH₵5,200. If the counter, the store, and the rules are specific to you, a custom POS and inventory build is GH₵7,000 to GH₵25,000.
