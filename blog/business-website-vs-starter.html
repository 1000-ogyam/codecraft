Starter website or business website?

Choose the starter at GH₵2,000 when you need a clear public page, a way to be contacted, and the first year of domain, SSL, and hosting. Choose the business website at GH₵4,500 when the company needs more pages for services, proof, and a fuller content setup.

Revisions and support

A starter includes two revision rounds. A business website includes three. Included support after handover is 30 days on a starter and 3 months on a business website. That support covers defects in the agreed scope, not a new feature.

When neither is enough

A corporate site of about 15 pages is GH₵7,000. A membership site starts at GH₵8,000. If people must log in and follow a process, look at a portal, from GH₵10,000, rather than stretching a brochure site.
