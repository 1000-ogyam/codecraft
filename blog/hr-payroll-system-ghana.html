HR and payroll systems

HR and payroll starts at GH₵12,000, excluding tax. The figure moves with headcount, how many locations employ people, and which reports finance already expects. It is not a spreadsheet with a login pasted on top unless that is truly all you need.

Treat the data as sensitive

Salaries and personal files are scoped with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) in mind. The quotation should name who can see pay, and how a leaver’s access is removed.

Training is part of the system

Custom systems include team training and a user guide, plus three months of post-launch support. If you also want a classroom day for a wider team, staff training is GH₵1,000 for a half day and GH₵1,800 for a full day.
