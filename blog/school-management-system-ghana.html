School management systems in Ghana

A school system has to hold students, classes, fees, and the people allowed to see each record. The published starting price is GH₵15,000, excluding tax. The real figure follows the number of campuses, how fees are taken, and whether parents must log in.

Why discovery is paid at this size

Systems of GH₵15,000 and above start with technical discovery from GH₵1,000. That fee is credited in full if the school proceeds within 30 days. The session is where fee rules, report cards, and who may edit a record get written down.

Personal data

A school holds personal data. The build is scoped with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) in mind. The quotation should say who can see a pupil’s record, and how a parent asks for a correction.
