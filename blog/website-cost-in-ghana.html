How much does a website cost in Ghana?

A starter website or starter online store from Codecraft Technologies is GH₵2,000. A business website is GH₵4,500. A premium store is GH₵8,500. Those figures are starting prices in Ghana cedis and exclude tax. Year-one domain, SSL, and hosting are included on the starter and business website.

What sits around that price

An extra page is GH₵200. A landing page is GH₵2,000. A booking or appointment website is GH₵6,000. A professional corporate website is GH₵7,000. A membership or directory site starts at GH₵8,000, and a custom portal starts at GH₵10,000.

What moves the quote

Pages, how custom the design is, payments such as mobile money or Paystack, who writes the content, languages, user roles, and how fast you need it. Payment-provider fees are paid by the business and are not part of the build price. Work starts after a written quote and a cleared deposit.
