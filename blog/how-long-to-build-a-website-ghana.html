How long does a website take in Ghana?

A business website commonly takes a few weeks from kick-off once the content is available. More complex shops and platforms take longer because products, payments, and roles take decisions, not because the calendar is padded. The quotation states the timeline for your scope.

Published ranges for systems

  • A CRM is about 4 to 8 weeks.
  • A manufacturing system is typically 8 to 16 weeks.
  • A business management suite is typically 3 to 6 months.

Your delay moves the date. Send content within 7 days of the deposit, and feedback within 5 business days of each review. A rush, meaning delivery in less than half the usual time, is a premium of 25 to 50 percent, and only if we have capacity.
