What an online store costs in Ghana

The public starting prices are simple. A starter online store is GH₵2,000. A premium store is GH₵8,500. Prices exclude tax. Extra products beyond the package limit are GH₵5 each.

Premium is a larger shop, not a different company

The premium store is the one to read when you expect many products, customer accounts, and a shop you will run every week. If several sellers must each have a stall, or checkout must talk to stock software, that is advanced e-commerce, from GH₵12,000, and it is quoted after the brief.

Costs that are not the build

Delivery, packaging, the payment provider’s fee, and the advertising budget are yours. Care plans, if you want the shop looked after after launch, start at GH₵250 a month.
