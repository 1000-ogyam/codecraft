What happens on the free first call

The first call is free for up to 30 minutes. It is remote wherever possible. Use it to say what the business sells, what the website or system must do, and what you already tried. You leave with a recommendation: a package, a scoped quotation, or a discovery session.

What the call is not

It is not a design presentation and it is not on-site. An on-site visit is from GH₵400 plus transport, confirmed before anyone travels. Systems under GH₵15,000 can include a free discovery session of up to two hours. Larger or complex systems start with paid technical discovery from GH₵1,000, credited if you proceed within 30 days.
