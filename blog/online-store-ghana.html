How to open an online store in Ghana

An online store in Ghana starts as a catalogue a customer can browse on a phone, with a checkout you have agreed in writing. At CodeCraft the starter online store is GH₵2,000. The premium store is GH₵8,500. Prices exclude tax.

What to decide before the build

  • Who delivers, and which cities you actually serve.
  • Whether the customer pays online, on delivery, or both.
  • Who writes the product names, prices, and photos.
  • Whether stock is counted in the store or in another system.

Payments

A gateway that is not already in the package starts at GH₵2,000. The provider’s own fees stay with the business. A store that also needs multi-vendor checkout or a link to stock software is an advanced e-commerce build, from GH₵12,000.
