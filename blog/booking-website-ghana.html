Booking and appointment websites in Ghana

There are two different jobs. A booking website, with online scheduling, confirmations, and reminders, is GH₵6,000. A booking and reservation system with rooms, resources, or staff rules starts at GH₵12,000. Prices exclude tax.

Pick the smaller one when

One location, one calendar, and a customer who chooses a time and leaves a name. Reminders can be part of that website quote. A payment at the time of booking is a gateway, from GH₵2,000 if it is not already included.

Pick the system when

Several staff, several rooms, deposits, or a rule that Tuesday afternoons are blocked. That is software, with discovery, a demo, training, and a user guide. The timeline is quoted, not guessed on a sales call.
