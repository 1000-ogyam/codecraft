Before you approve a quote

Payment, scope, and handover

Currency and tax

Quotations are in Ghana cedis. List prices exclude taxes unless the quotation says otherwise. Third-party services billed in another currency are quoted at the rate on the quotation date.

How you pay

Up to GH₵5,000: 70% deposit, 30% before handover. GH₵5,001–15,000: 60%, then 30% at a milestone, then 10% before handover. GH₵15,001–50,000: 50%, 30%, and 20%. Above GH₵50,000, milestones are written into the contract. Nothing starts until the deposit has cleared, and handover waits until the agreed balance is paid.

Retainers and renewals

Marketing, maintenance, and support plans are billed monthly in advance and are due by the 5th. Domain and hosting renewals are billed at least 30 days before expiry. Standard package quotations are valid for 30 days. Custom work and retainers are valid for 14 days.

Scope and changes

The quotation states what is included. Starter sites include two revision rounds. A Business Website includes three. Extra pages, features, integrations, or revision rounds are written up and approved before that work starts. If you need delivery in less than half the usual time, a rush premium of 25–50% applies, and only if we have capacity.

After go-live

Every project has 30 days of defect fixes for the agreed scope. Included support is 30 days for Starter Website and Starter Online Store, 3 months for a Business Website and for custom systems, and 6 months for Premium E-Commerce. That support covers defects, security updates, how-to guidance, and minor content edits of up to 2 hours a month. It does not cover new features.

Ownership

On full payment you own the final content, branding, and data, and you receive a licence to use the website or system. CodeCraft keeps its existing tools and reusable components. A footer credit is standard unless we agree otherwise in writing. Source-code ownership can be transferred on request and is quoted per project. Credentials and source files are handed over after the agreed payment is received.

If you cancel after work has started, the deposit covers reserved time and work already done, and committed third-party costs are still payable. A project with no reply for 30 days is paused. After 60 days it may be closed. Send content within 7 days of the deposit, and feedback within 5 business days of each review. Your delay moves the delivery date by the same amount.
