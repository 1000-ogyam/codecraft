Our Story

Engineering Growth
Across West Africa

Founded in 2019, Codecraft Technologies (operating locally and widely known as CodeCraft Ghana) is a premier, legally registered full-service digital engineering agency based in Accra, Ghana. We bridge the gap between creative design and robust technical execution to help ambitious small and medium enterprises (SMEs) scale across West Africa.

As a forward-thinking software firm, Codecraft Technologies specializes in delivering custom web design, secure mobile applications, localized fintech integrations, and results-driven digital marketing strategies. We don't just build software; we engineer tools that solve real-world operational challenges. This includes our proprietary cloud platform, ProDesk24, an all-in-one CRM and business management ecosystem tailored specifically for the African startup ecosystem to streamline invoicing, task tracking, and customer relations.

From our central office at GM-108 Adam Street in Accra, CodeCraft Ghana has successfully deployed over 80 digital projects for a diverse portfolio of more than 100 clients—including prominent organizations like the Association of Ghana Industries (AGI). With a certified 97% on-time project delivery rate, our team combines global development standards with deep regional market expertise to deliver software that truly moves the needle for your business.
